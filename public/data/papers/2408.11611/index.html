DTN: Deep Multiple Task-specific Feature Interactions Network for Multi-Task Recommendation

Authors: Yaowen Bi, Yuteng Lian, Jie Cui, Jun Liu, Peijian Wang, Guanghui Li, Xuejun Chen, Jinglin Zhao, Hao Wen, Jing Zhang, Zhaoqi Zhang, Wenzhuo Song, Yang Sun, Weiwei Zhang, Mingchen Cai, Jian Dong, Guanxing Zhang

Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.11611

Generated summary artifact for 2408.11611

Thesis

The Deep Multiple Task-specific Feature Interactions Network (DTN), by integrating diversified task-specific feature interaction methods and a task-sensitive network, can effectively learn nuanced task-specific feature interaction representations, thereby enhancing the efficiency of joint representation learning and outperforming state-of-the-art Multi-Task Learning models in recommendation system performance.

Core Problem

Existing Multi-Task Learning (MTL) models for recommender systems (e.g., MMoE, PLE) fail to explicitly consider feature interactions during optimization, which are crucial for capturing complex high-order features. Furthermore, the importance of the same feature can diverge significantly across different tasks in MTL, leading to suboptimal performance.

Key Novelty

A novel Deep Multiple Task-specific Feature Interactions Network (DTN) that incorporates diversified task-specific feature interaction methods and a task-sensitive network to learn nuanced task-specific representations in Multi-Task Learning for recommendation.

Key Contributions

  • DTN achieved significant improvements in online A/B testing on Shopee's E-commerce recommender system, with +3.28% in clicks, +3.10% in orders, and +2.70% in Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) compared to state-of-the-art MTL models.
  • DTN consistently outperformed strong MTL baselines like MMoE and PLE across industrial (Shopee) and various public benchmark datasets (Census-Income, AE, Ali-CCP) in terms of AUC and LogLoss metrics.
  • The model demonstrated the highest metric uplifts (RelaImp) for CTR, ATC, and CVR prediction tasks on the Shopee industrial dataset (e.g., +6.06% RelaImp for CTR).
  • Visualization experiments confirmed that DTN effectively learns diversified task-specific feature interaction representations, enhancing the model's ability to capture nuanced relationships.

Results

  • The proposed DTN model consistently outperforms state-of-the-art MTL models (MMoE, PLE) across various industrial and public benchmark datasets, demonstrating its effectiveness in recommendation tasks.
  • DTN achieves significant uplifts in real-world online A/B tests on Shopee's E-commerce platform, indicating its practical utility and impact on key business metrics like clicks, orders, and GMV.
  • The integration of diversified task-specific feature interaction modules and a task-sensitive network within DTN successfully addresses the challenge of varying feature importance across tasks, leading to improved and more efficient joint representation learning.

Limitations

  • The DTN model, while powerful, still has the potential to incorporate more additional effective feature interaction methods for further improvement.
  • The 'DTN-trim' version, which reduces about 9% of parameters compared to the full DTN, implies that the full model might be parameter-heavy, although overall parameter size was controlled for fair comparison (1.6E8 for various models).
