Top-n\sigma: Not All Logits Are You Need

Authors: Chenxia Tang, Jianchun Liu, Hongli Xu, Liusheng Huang

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07641

Generated summary artifact for 2411.07641

Thesis

By operating directly on pre-softmax logits and leveraging their inherent statistical properties—specifically, the distinction between Gaussian-distributed noisy regions and distinct informative regions—the top-n\sigma sampling method can achieve superior and temperature-invariant performance in reasoning tasks, consistently outperforming conventional sampling techniques and greedy decoding.

Core Problem

Large language models (LLMs) employing typical sampling methods for text generation in reasoning tasks face a trade-off between output diversity and accuracy, often leading to inconsistent performance, particularly at higher temperatures, due to their inability to effectively filter irrelevant tokens from pre-softmax logits.

Key Novelty

Top-n\sigma, a novel logit-based sampling method that leverages statistical properties of pre-softmax logits to dynamically filter noisy tokens, ensuring temperature-invariant and computationally efficient generation, and consistently outperforming existing methods in reasoning tasks.

Key Contributions

  • Top-n\sigma consistently outperforms greedy decoding (temperature = 0.0) across all evaluated reasoning datasets.
  • The method demonstrates exceptional stability and robust performance even at high temperatures (e.g., T=3.0), where standard sampling methods and top-p completely fail (0.00% EM).
  • Results suggest that moderately higher temperatures (e.g., T=1.5), when combined with top-n\sigma, can yield optimal performance for reasoning tasks, challenging the conventional preference for low-temperature decoding.
  • In multiple-pass evaluations (Maj@20), top-n\sigma maintains strong exploration capabilities and delivers competitive or superior performance compared to other sampling methods.

Results

  • Top-n\sigma consistently outperforms greedy decoding and other stochastic sampling methods (top-p, top-k, min-p) across various reasoning-focused datasets.
  • The method demonstrates exceptional robustness and stable performance even at high temperatures, effectively addressing the degradation seen in other methods.
  • Optimal performance for reasoning tasks is often achieved at moderately higher temperatures (around 1.5) when using top-n\sigma, rather than low-temperature or greedy decoding.
  • Top-n\sigma maintains effective exploration capabilities, as evidenced by strong performance in multiple-pass (Maj@20) evaluations, further challenging the trade-off perception between diversity and accuracy.

Limitations

  • The exact mixing mechanism of distributions in the logit space remains unknown, even though theoretical bounds are provided for Gaussian and uniform approximations.
  • The paper primarily focuses on inference-time improvements, with insights regarding noise sources suggesting potential future work in improving model architecture and training procedures.
  • The theoretical bounds for the threshold multiplier $n$ are $(0, 2\sqrt{3}) \approx (0, 3.46)$, with $n=1.0$ being a practical recommendation; exceeding this range might include noisy tokens and degrade performance.
