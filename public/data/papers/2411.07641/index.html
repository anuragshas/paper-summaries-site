Top-nsigma: Not All Logits Are You Need

Authors: Chenxia Tang, Jianchun Liu, Hongli Xu, Liusheng Huang

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07641

Generated summary artifact for 2411.07641

Thesis

The paper posits that by analyzing pre-softmax logit distributions, one can identify distinct noisy and informative regions. Leveraging this insight, the proposed top-nsigma sampling method can efficiently filter irrelevant tokens, achieving superior and temperature-invariant performance for LLM reasoning tasks, even outperforming traditional greedy decoding methods.

Core Problem

Current Large Language Model (LLM) sampling methods for reasoning tasks often exhibit a trade-off between diversity and accuracy, struggling to maintain reasoning quality, especially at varying temperatures. This stems from their inability to effectively filter irrelevant or noisy tokens from the pre-softmax logits.

Key Novelty

A novel, logit-based sampling method called top-nsigma that leverages the statistical properties of pre-softmax logit distributions to dynamically filter noisy tokens, resulting in temperature-invariant, efficient, and superior performance for reasoning tasks.

Key Contributions

  • top-nsigma consistently outperforms existing sampling methods (top-p, top-k, min-p, temperature sampling) across four diverse reasoning-focused datasets (AQuA, MATH, GSM8K, GPQA).
  • It surpasses greedy decoding (temperature = 0.0) in all evaluated reasoning tasks, challenging the conventional wisdom that deterministic methods are best for reasoning.
  • The method demonstrates exceptional stability and robust performance even at high temperatures (e.g., T=3.0), where other methods catastrophically degrade.
  • top-nsigma combined with moderately higher temperatures (around T=1.5) yields optimal performance, suggesting that controlled exploration can enhance reasoning quality.

Results

  • top-nsigma consistently achieves the best or near-best performance across all datasets in single-pass evaluation, outperforming other sampling methods.
  • The stochastic sampling approach of top-nsigma challenges conventional wisdom by outperforming greedy (deterministic) decoding across all datasets for reasoning tasks.
  • top-nsigma demonstrates robust performance at higher temperatures (e.g., T=3.0), where conventional methods suffer catastrophic degradation, maintaining significantly higher accuracy.
  • Experiments suggest that moderately higher temperatures (typically around 1.5) combined with top-nsigma lead to optimal performance, indicating that controlled exploration enhances reasoning quality.

Limitations

  • The paper focuses on inference-time sampling and does not fundamentally address the root causes of noise (e.g., training data noise, regularization effects, softmax limitations) during model training, although it suggests insights could improve future training procedures.
  • This is a preprint version and is explicitly stated as incomplete, with missing experimental results and appendices to be supplemented later.
