Toward Autonomous Long-horizon Engineering for ML Research

Authors: Guoxin Chen, Jie Chen, Lei Chen, Jiale Zhao, Fanzhe Meng, Wayne Xin Zhao, Ruihua Song, Cheng Chen, Ji-Rong Wen and Kai Jia

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/arXiv:2604.13018

Generated summary artifact for 2604.13018

Thesis

AiScientist addresses the challenge of autonomous long-horizon ML research engineering by combining hierarchical orchestration with a permission-scoped File-as-Bus workspace, enabling agents to sustain coherent progress across complex, multi-stage iterative tasks through structured coordination and durable state continuity.

Core Problem

Long-horizon ML research engineering remains difficult for autonomous AI agents, as they must sustain coherent progress across task comprehension, environment setup, implementation, experimentation, and debugging over hours or days.

Key Novelty

AiScientist introduces a novel system design for autonomous long-horizon ML research engineering, combining hierarchical orchestration with artifact-mediated coordination via a "thin control over thick state" principle to ensure durable state continuity.

Key Contributions

  • AiScientist improves PaperBench average score by 10.54 points over the best matched baseline.
  • AiScientist achieves 81.82 Any Medal% on MLE-Bench Lite, exceeding all reported leaderboard results.
  • The File-as-Bus protocol is a key driver of performance, with its removal reducing PaperBench score by 6.41 points and MLE-Bench Lite Any Medal% by 31.82 points.
  • Hierarchical orchestration, alongside durable state continuity, is crucial for strong long-horizon performance.

Results

  • AiScientist achieves substantial gains on PaperBench (33.73 average score, +11.15 points over baseline) and MLE-Bench Lite (81.82% Any Medal%, +18.18% over baseline), narrowing the gap to human performance.
  • The system is more cost-effective than IterativeAgent, achieving higher scores at significantly lower costs per task.
  • Durable state continuity, mediated by the File-as-Bus protocol, is critical for long-horizon ML research engineering performance, particularly for later-round refinement.
  • Hierarchical orchestration, beyond simple agent interaction, plays a material role in improving performance for complex, multi-stage tasks.

Limitations

  • Not specified
