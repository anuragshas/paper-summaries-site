Top-H Decoding: Adapting the Creativity and Coherence with Bounded Entropy in Text Generation

Authors: Erfan Baghaei Potraghloo, Seyedarmin Azizi, Souvik Kundu, and Massoud Pedram

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02510

Generated summary artifact for 2509.02510

Thesis

Top-H decoding provides a theoretically grounded and computationally efficient greedy algorithm to balance creativity and coherence in open-ended text generation, outperforming existing sampling methods, especially at high temperatures, by addressing the entropy-constrained mass maximization problem.

Core Problem

Large language models (LLMs) struggle to balance two competing objectives in open-ended text generation: fostering diversity and creativity while preserving logical coherence. Existing truncation-based sampling techniques (e.g., temperature scaling, top-p, min-p) often fail to effectively incorporate the model's confidence, leading to suboptimal trade-offs, particularly at higher temperatures where coherence degrades.

Key Novelty

Top-H decoding, a novel sampling method that dynamically selects a subset of tokens for text generation by maintaining minimal divergence from the original distribution under an upper-bounded entropy constraint, thereby balancing creativity and coherence.

Key Contributions

  • Top-H consistently outperforms existing sampling methods (min-p, top-p, top-k, eta-sampling) in balancing creativity and coherence.
  • Demonstrates remarkable robustness to temperature scaling, preserving effectiveness at high temperatures where traditional methods significantly degrade.
  • Achieves up to 25.63% higher accuracy compared to min-p on creative writing benchmarks with LLaMA3.1-8B.
  • Maintains strong accuracy on reasoning datasets (GPQA, GSM8K) even at high temperatures, with significant accuracy improvements over baselines.
  • Exhibits negligible computational overhead, performing as efficiently as top-p and min-p (as low as 0.8% overhead).
  • Outperforms Mirostat, another entropy-aware baseline, by +13.3% on MT-Bench and +4.3% on GPQA averaged across models and temperatures.

Results

  • Top-H decoding consistently outperforms established sampling methods (min-p, top-p, top-k, eta-sampling) in balancing creativity and coherence across various LLMs and tasks.
  • The method demonstrates superior robustness to temperature scaling, maintaining high performance at increased temperatures where other baselines experience significant degradation.
  • Top-H provides substantial accuracy improvements on reasoning and QA benchmarks (e.g., up to 25.63% on creative writing benchmarks, +7.36% on GPQA) and achieves higher human-aligned evaluation scores for creative writing.
  • Top-H introduces negligible computational overhead, making it a practically efficient solution despite addressing an NP-hard problem.
  • The greedy approach of Top-H is empirically shown to closely approximate the optimal solution to the entropy-constrained mass maximization problem.

Limitations

  • As a greedy solution to an NP-hard problem, Top-H does not provide general competitive guarantees that apply across a broad range of distributions.
  • The hyperparameter alpha, which controls the entropy threshold, is currently tuned manually, though the method exhibits robustness to its variation.
  • Future research is needed to design an algorithm that offers a provable approximation ratio and can dynamically adapt the entropy threshold alpha.
