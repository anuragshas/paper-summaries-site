p-LESS SAMPLING: A Robust Hyperparameter-Free Approach for LLM Decoding

Authors: Runyan Tan, Shuang Wu, Phillip Howard

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23234

Generated summary artifact for 2509.23234

Thesis

p-less sampling is a novel hyperparameter-free decoding strategy for LLMs that dynamically adjusts its truncation threshold based on information-theoretic principles derived from the entire token probability distribution, yielding robust, high-quality, and efficient text generation across diverse tasks and temperatures.

Core Problem

Obtaining high-quality and robust outputs from Large Language Models (LLMs) via sampling-based decoding is challenging because existing methods are sensitive to hyperparameter choices that depend on the generation task and temperature settings, often leading to text degeneration or requiring extensive tuning.

Key Novelty

A robust, hyperparameter-free sampling strategy (p-less sampling) for LLM decoding, which dynamically adapts its truncation threshold based on information-theoretic principles from the entire token probability distribution.

Key Contributions

  • p-less and its variant p-less_norm consistently achieve the highest or second-highest Area Under the Curve (AUC) on math and logical reasoning datasets for Llama2-7b, Mistral-7b, and Llama3-70b.
  • p-less performs significantly better than other methods in creative writing tasks at higher temperatures, demonstrating robust quality (e.g., win rate +16.70 for Llama-2-7b at \$\tau=2.0\$ over min-p).
  • p-less achieves the fastest average sampling speed per token (0.01942 s), representing a 22% reduction compared to the min-p baseline.
  • Human evaluation confirmed a preference for p-less generated stories over alternative sampling approaches for creative writing.

Results

  • p-less consistently outperforms existing sampling approaches in math and logical reasoning tasks, especially at higher temperatures where other methods degrade.
  • It maintains superior performance in creative writing tasks, avoiding quality degradation typically seen in other methods at higher temperatures.
  • p-less is more inference-time efficient, achieving faster average token sampling speeds and shorter generation lengths without sacrificing accuracy.
  • The method is robust, hyperparameter-free, and grounded in information theory, providing a principled approach to LLM decoding.
