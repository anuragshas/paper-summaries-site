Dual-Rerank: Fusing Sequential Dependencies and Utility for Generative Reranking

Authors: Chao Zhang, Shuai Lin, Chenglei Dai, Ye Qian, Mingyang Fan, Yi Zhang, Yi Wang, Jingwei Zhuo

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/arXiv:2604.07420

Generated summary artifact for 2604.07420

Thesis

Dual-Rerank effectively resolves the accuracy-latency and imitation-evolution dualities in industrial reranking by combining sequential knowledge distillation from an AR teacher to a NAR student with stable online reinforcement learning (LDRO) for whole-page utility optimization, achieving state-of-the-art performance with reduced latency.

Core Problem

Industrial reranking faces a fundamental 'dual dilemma': 1) a structural trade-off where Autoregressive (AR) models offer superior sequential modeling but suffer from prohibitive O(L) latency, while Non-Autoregressive (NAR) models offer O(1) efficiency but lack dependency capturing. 2) An optimization gap where Supervised Learning (SL) struggles to directly optimize whole-page utility, while Reinforcement Learning (RL) suffers from instability in high-throughput data streams.

Key Novelty

A unified online framework named Dual-Rerank that resolves the accuracy-latency tension and optimization gap in industrial reranking by combining Sequential Knowledge Distillation and List-wise Decoupled Reranking Optimization (LDRO).

Key Contributions

  • Achieved State-of-the-Art performance on both public (Avito Context Ad Clicks) and industrial (Kuaishou Production Dataset) benchmarks, outperforming NAR baselines by a substantial margin and matching/exceeding AR teachers.
  • Drastically reduced average inference latency by 43.7% (from 21.5ms for Production AR to 12.1ms for Dual-Rerank), converting 'saved latency' into expanded 'search breadth' for better global optimality.
  • Demonstrated statistically significant gains in crucial online business metrics during A/B testing on the Kuaishou App, including +1.107% Long-View Rate, +0.714% Whole-Page CTR, and -1.309% Query Reformulation Rate.
  • Provided theoretical justification via the 'Unimodal Concentration Hypothesis' and the 'epsilon-Bound Ranking Stability Theorem' for the feasibility and stability of transferring sequential knowledge from AR to NAR models in reranking tasks.

Results

  • Dual-Rerank significantly outperforms existing NAR baselines and matches or exceeds the strongest AR teachers on AUC and NDCG metrics across diverse datasets.
  • Sequential Knowledge Distillation (Phase 1) is foundational for transferring AR's sequential dependency logic to the NAR student, effectively preventing 'Ranking Collapse' and ensuring structural fidelity.
  • List-wise Decoupled Reranking Optimization (LDRO) (Phase 2) is crucial for stable online RL, effectively handling non-stationary reward shifts and leading to superior performance compared to standard GRPO.
  • The framework drastically reduces inference latency (43.7% reduction) while simultaneously improving core business metrics and user satisfaction in real-world online A/B tests.

Limitations

  • The paper does not explicitly list limitations, but suggests that future work should explore scaling this paradigm to foundation models to exploit emergent list-wise reasoning abilities, implying a current scope limitation regarding advanced reasoning.
