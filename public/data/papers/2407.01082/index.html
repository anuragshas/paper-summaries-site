TURNING UP THE HEAT: MIN-p SAMPLING FOR CREATIVE AND COHERENT LLM OUTPUTS

Authors: Nguyen Nhat Minh, Andrew Baker, Clement Neo, Allen Roush, Andreas Kirsch, Ravid Shwartz-Ziv

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01082

Generated summary artifact for 2407.01082

Thesis

Min-p sampling, a novel dynamic truncation method, effectively balances creativity and coherence in Large Language Model (LLM) generated text, particularly at high temperatures, by dynamically adjusting the sampling threshold based on the model's confidence.

Core Problem

The central challenge in Large Language Models (LLMs) is the trade-off between generating creative and diverse text outputs while maintaining coherence and quality, especially at higher decoding temperatures.

Key Novelty

Min-p sampling, a novel dynamic truncation method for LLMs that adjusts the sampling threshold based on the model's confidence by scaling a base probability threshold with the top token's probability.

Key Contributions

  • Min-p sampling consistently achieved higher accuracy on graduate-level reasoning (GPQA) and grade school math (GSM8K CoT) benchmarks across most temperature settings, demonstrating robustness and improved correctness, especially at higher temperatures.
  • The method significantly outperformed baseline methods, including top-p, on the AlpacaEval Creative Writing benchmark, yielding higher win rates and length-controlled win rates for creative and engaging text.
  • Human evaluations showed a clear preference for min-p outputs in terms of both quality and diversity, particularly at higher temperature settings, validating its real-world utility.
  • Min-p demonstrated a superior accuracy-diversity trade-off compared to top-p, with its configurations consistently lying closer to the Pareto frontier, indicating more efficient performance.
  • The benefits of min-p sampling generalized well across diverse LLM families and scales, from 1B to 123B parameters (Mistral and Llama 3 models), highlighting its broad applicability.

Results

  • Min-p sampling consistently achieves higher accuracy on graduate-level reasoning and grade school math benchmarks across most temperature settings, demonstrating improved correctness and robustness at higher temperatures.
  • Min-p significantly outperforms other methods in creative writing tasks (AlpacaEval) by achieving higher win rates and length-controlled win rates.
  • Human evaluations show a clear preference for min-p generated text in terms of both quality and diversity, especially in high-temperature settings.
  • The method demonstrates a better accuracy-diversity trade-off compared to top-p sampling, with its configurations closer to the Pareto frontier, indicating more efficient performance.
  • Min-p's benefits generalize well across various LLM families and sizes (1B to 123B parameters) and integrates seamlessly into existing inference pipelines with minimal computational overhead.

Limitations

  • The initial human evaluation had limitations regarding sample size and the assessment of diversity for other potential triplets from the same condition.
  • Potential misuse exists where min-p could enhance the fluency of misleading or harmful content, emphasizing the need for responsible implementation and content filtering.
  • High-temperature text generation using min-p could potentially increase risks of circumventing safety finetuning, though no such instances were observed in practice.
