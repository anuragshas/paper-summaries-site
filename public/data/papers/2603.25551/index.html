Thesis

Voxtral TTS is an expressive multilingual text-to-speech model that generates natural speech from short audio references by combining an auto-regressive semantic token generator with a flow-matching acoustic token generator, leveraging a novel speech tokenizer (Voxtral Codec) and Direct Preference Optimization for improved quality and expressivity, achieving state-of-the-art human preference and efficient production deployment.

Core Problem

Generating natural and expressive text-to-speech, particularly for multilingual zero-shot voice cloning, while maintaining strong intelligibility and accurately capturing speech nuances and expressivity from short audio references.

Key Novelty

A multilingual zero-shot TTS system with a novel hybrid architecture (auto-regressive semantic tokens + flow-matching acoustic tokens), a custom speech tokenizer (Voxtral Codec), and adapted Direct Preference Optimization, achieving state-of-the-art performance in human evaluations and production-ready inference.

Key Contributions

  • Voxtral TTS significantly outperforms ElevenLabs Flash v2.5 in human evaluations for multilingual zero-shot voice cloning, achieving an overall 68.4% win rate.
  • The model demonstrates competitive performance against strong proprietary systems like ElevenLabs v3 and Gemini 2.5 Flash TTS in flagship-voice evaluations across various emotion steering settings.
  • Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) post-training generally improves objective metrics like Word Error Rate (WER) and UTMOS, while qualitatively reducing hallucinations and improving volume consistency.
  • CUDA graph acceleration for the flow-matching transformer reduces latency by 47% and improves Real-Time Factor (RTF) by 2.5x, enabling production-ready performance.
  • The system achieves high inference throughput (12x increase in characters/second/GPU) and sub-second time to first audio with asynchronous chunked streaming, supporting over 30 concurrent users on a single H200 GPU.

Results

  • Voxtral Codec outperforms the Mimi codec on objective metrics, demonstrating superior reconstruction quality and intelligibility at similar bitrates.
  • Voxtral TTS achieves strong preference in human evaluations for multilingual zero-shot voice cloning, significantly surpassing ElevenLabs Flash v2.5 across multiple languages.
  • The adapted Direct Preference Optimization effectively enhances speech quality, reducing artifacts and improving consistency in pretrained models.
  • The combination of CUDA graph acceleration and asynchronous chunked streaming enables Voxtral TTS to achieve sub-second first-audio latency and high throughput, making it suitable for production environments.
  • The hybrid architecture, combining auto-regressive generation for semantic tokens and flow-matching for acoustic tokens, provides a robust and expressive TTS system.

Limitations

  • Automated metrics like UTMOS were found to be loose proxies for naturalness and expressivity, poorly calibrated across languages, and weakly correlated with human preference.
  • Higher values of the Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) scale ($\alpha$) during inference can lead to over-adherence to the provided voice prompt, causing the model to fail in biasing towards implicit emotions from the text prompt.
  • Training the Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) model for longer durations on synthetic data was observed to result in more robotic-sounding speech.
